# Data Processing Agreement (draft template)

> DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW. A starting point for counsel, structured after GDPR art. 28. Not signed by anyone. For EU
> customers counsel will likely attach the EU Standard Contractual Clauses where data leaves the EEA (e.g. support
> access, US AI providers) and add UK and Swiss addenda if needed; for US customers, the CCPA service-provider terms.

**Between** the customer (the "Controller") and `[COMPANY LEGAL NAME]`, `[COMPANY ADDRESS]`, `[REGISTRATION NUMBER]`
(the "Processor"). Effective `[EFFECTIVE DATE]`, part of the CostGuard subscription agreement.

## 1. Subject matter and duration
The Processor processes personal data on behalf of the Controller to provide CostGuard (control of AI-agent
spending, software subscriptions, licenses and company equipment) for the term of the subscription and the deletion
period after it (section 9).

## 2. Nature and purpose
Storage, organisation, analysis and display of the data the Controller enters or connects; matching of license and
device holders; anomaly detection on agent spending; notifications; forwarding of agent prompts to AI providers the
Controller enables.

## 3. Categories of data subjects and data
- Data subjects: the Controller's employees and contractors; the Controller's users of CostGuard.
- Data: name, work e-mail, position, department, employment/dismissal dates, licenses held and last sign-in dates in
  connected systems, devices held, requests (vacation, purchase, license), payroll payment notices if the Controller
  uses that feature, documents the Controller uploads, actions of users, contents of agent prompts.
- No special categories are intended. The Controller must not upload them.

## 4. Instructions
The Processor processes only on documented instructions of the Controller (this DPA, the configuration the Controller
makes in CostGuard), unless required by law, and informs the Controller if an instruction infringes data protection law.

## 5. Confidentiality and security
Staff with access are bound by confidentiality. Technical and organisational measures: Annex 2
([SECURITY_PRIVACY_OVERVIEW.md](SECURITY_PRIVACY_OVERVIEW.md)).

## 6. Subprocessors
General authorisation for the subprocessors in [SUBPROCESSORS.md](SUBPROCESSORS.md). `[30]` days' notice of changes;
the Controller may object on reasonable grounds; Processor remains liable for its subprocessors.

## 7. Assistance
The Processor assists with data subject requests (export, rectification, erasure features —
[GDPR_DSR_PROCEDURE.md](GDPR_DSR_PROCEDURE.md)), with DPIAs and prior consultations, and with security.

## 8. Personal data breaches
Notification to the Controller without undue delay and within `[48]` hours of becoming aware, with the information of
art. 33(3) available at the time. Counsel: confirm the hours.

## 9. Deletion or return
At the end of the subscription the Controller may export its data; the Processor deletes the company's data
`[30]` days after the end (company erasure, ADR 0021), and backups as they expire (`[30]` days).

## 10. Audits
The Processor makes available information necessary to demonstrate compliance (this documentation, reports of
independent audits once they exist — none exists yet) and allows audits `[once a year, 30 days' notice, at the
Controller's cost]`.

## 11. Location and transfers
Data is hosted in the AWS region of the Controller's installation (US or EU). Transfers outside the EEA only with a
transfer mechanism (SCCs, adequacy, DPF) — Annex 3.

## 12. Liability, law
`[GOVERNING LAW]`, `[COURTS]`; liability as in the main agreement.

**Annex 1** — details of processing (sections 1–3). **Annex 2** — security measures. **Annex 3** — transfers and SCCs.
